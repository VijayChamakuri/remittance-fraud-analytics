Why the aggressive point is the right call: a missed fraud costs ~the transaction value (avg ~$161) while a false positive costs ~$4 of review friction — an implied ~40.3:1 cost ratio. At that ratio it is rational to accept ~40 false alarms to stop one fraud, so the cost-minimizing threshold sits at high recall.

Root-cause analysis

Top drivers are 1-hour velocity, device change, new-corridor and anonymized network signals. The model catches account-takeover well but under-catches mule fraud, which hides in normal new-account behavior. → full RCA writeup.
